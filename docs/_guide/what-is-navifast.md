---
title: What is Navifast?
description: A native Navidrome client in development.
nav_order: 1
---

Navifast is a native desktop client written in Rust with Rune for your own Navidrome server. It is a
fork of the upstream native Spotify client, retaining its UI foundation and
audio DSP while replacing service-specific behavior.

The initial build connects to your server, browses albums and plays their
songs locally with transport controls, an equalizer and spectrum display.
It has no browser engine, telemetry or hosted backend. No Spotify account is
needed. See [Getting started](/getting-started/) for the implemented scope.
